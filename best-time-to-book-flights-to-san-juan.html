Flying to San Juan from the US

San Juan has extensive nonstop service from dozens of US cities on JetBlue, Spirit, Frontier, American, Delta, and United — it's essentially treated as a domestic route since Puerto Rico is a US territory. Flight times run roughly 2.5 to 4.5 hours.

Winter (December–April) is dry season and the busiest, priciest stretch. Hurricane season (June–November, peak risk August–October) is cheaper with storm risk; late spring and fall shoulder months offer good value.

Budget vs. premium carriers on this route

JetBlue, Spirit, and Frontier typically post the lowest headline fares; American, Delta, and United can be competitive in the middle of the optimal booking window.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to San Juan for the cheapest fare?

2 to 3 weeks out on budget carriers, or 3 to 5 weeks out on full-service carriers.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to San Juan?

Hurricane season (June–November) is generally cheaper. Winter (December–April) is dry season and the most expensive.

Is it cheaper to fly to San Juan on a budget or full-service airline?

JetBlue, Spirit, and Frontier often have the lowest headline fares; full-service carriers can be competitive if booked within the optimal window.

Do I need a passport to visit San Juan from the US?

No passport or visa is required — Puerto Rico is a US territory, so flying to San Juan is treated like any other domestic US flight.
