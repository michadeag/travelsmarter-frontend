Frequently asked questions

Does Thailand charge a tourist tax?

Thailand charges a tourist/accommodation tax nationwide. Intended to fund tourist infrastructure and insurance; applies regardless of city.

How much is the tourist tax in Thailand?

A tourist entry fee of around 300 THB.

How do I pay the tourist tax in Thailand?

Collection method has changed over time — check current status before travel, as it has been delayed/adjusted multiple times.

Is the tourist tax included in the price shown when I book in Thailand?

Almost never — tourist and accommodation taxes are typically excluded from the headline price shown by booking sites and added separately at check-in or check-out, so it's worth budgeting a small buffer.
