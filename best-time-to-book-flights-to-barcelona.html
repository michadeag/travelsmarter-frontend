Flying to Barcelona from the US

Barcelona–El Prat (BCN) has nonstop service from JFK, EWR, MIA, and seasonally from ORD, flown by Level (IAG's budget transatlantic carrier), Delta, United, and American. Flight times run roughly 8.5 to 9 hours eastbound. Connections through Madrid or other European hubs open up more departure cities if a nonstop isn't available from your airport.

Peak season (June–September) is hot and heavily touristed, with the highest fares and long lines at Sagrada Familia and Park Güell. Shoulder season — April–May and October — offers milder weather, thinner crowds, and meaningfully lower fares. Winter (November–February) is cheapest overall.

Budget vs. premium carriers on this route

Level typically posts the lowest headline fares but sells out its cheapest inventory early, especially for summer travel. Full-service carriers hold back more seats and often beat Level's price if you book in the middle of your window rather than waiting for a last-minute deal.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to Barcelona for the cheapest fare?

4–6 weeks out on full-service carriers, or 3–5 weeks out on budget carriers. Book earlier within that window for summer travel, since demand is strong.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to Barcelona?

November–February is cheapest overall. April–May and October offer the best balance of price and weather.

Is it cheaper to fly to Barcelona on a budget or full-service airline?

Level often has the lowest headline fare, but full-service carriers can beat it if you book mid-window rather than last minute.

Do I need a visa to visit Barcelona from the US?

US citizens can currently visit visa-free for up to 90 days in the Schengen area. Check the current ETIAS requirement close to your travel date.
