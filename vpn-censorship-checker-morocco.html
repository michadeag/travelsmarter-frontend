Frequently asked questions

Is the internet censored in Morocco?

Morocco's internet censorship level is moderate — some specific services or content types are restricted, though most everyday apps work fine. VoIP calls (WhatsApp/Skype voice) have been restricted at various points, sometimes lifted and reinstated — text messaging generally still works.

Do I need a VPN for Morocco?

A VPN can help in Morocco for accessing specific restricted services, though most everyday apps work fine without one.

Is using a VPN legal in Morocco?

In Morocco, VPN legality is somewhat unclear in practice, though commonly used by travelers.

Should I set up my VPN before I arrive in Morocco?

It's good practice to set up and test your VPN before any trip, though it's less critical for Morocco specifically.
