Flying to Santorini from the US

Santorini (JTR) has no nonstop flights from the US — every itinerary connects through Athens or another European hub. Most travelers fly the transatlantic leg to Athens on Delta, American, or a European carrier, then take a short 45-minute domestic hop to Santorini on Aegean Airlines or Olympic Air. Total travel time typically runs 13 to 16 hours including connections.

July and August are Santorini's brutal peak — extreme heat, packed caldera-view sunset spots, and the highest fares and hotel prices of the year. Shoulder season (April–May and late September–October) is near-universally recommended: warm but manageable temperatures, thinner crowds, and noticeably better prices. Winter (November–March) is cheapest and quietest, though some hotels and restaurants close for the season.

Budget vs. premium carriers on this route

With no nonstop US service and no dedicated low-cost carrier, full-service pricing sets the cost on both the transatlantic leg to Athens and the short connecting flight to Santorini. Booking about a month out generally works best, since it gives you time to compare a few different Athens connections without waiting so long that fares on the popular domestic hop sell out.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to Santorini for the cheapest fare?

4–6 weeks out on full-service carriers, or 3–5 weeks out if your Athens connection has flexibility. Since every trip connects, total itinerary cost matters more than a single fare.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to Santorini?

November–March is cheapest and quietest. April–May and late September–October are the sweet spot — warm weather without the brutal heat and crowds of the July–August peak.

Is it cheaper to fly to Santorini on a budget or full-service airline?

There's no nonstop service or dedicated budget carrier — Delta or American fly to Athens, then Aegean or Olympic Air cover the short hop to Santorini. Full-service pricing dominates both legs.

Do I need a visa to visit Santorini from the US?

US citizens can visit Greece visa-free for up to 90 days in the Schengen area. Check the current ETIAS requirement before booking.
