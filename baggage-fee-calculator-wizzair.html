Frequently asked questions

How much does a checked bag cost on Wizz Air?

What is the weight limit for checked bags on Wizz Air?

Wizz Air's standard checked bag weight limit is 44lb. Over 20-23kg allowance: charged per kg, steep at the gate.

Is it cheaper to add a checked bag online or pay at the airport for Wizz Air?

Adding a checked bag during booking or online check-in is almost always cheaper than paying at the airport counter or gate — airport fees are typically higher across nearly all airlines, including Wizz Air.

Does Wizz Air include a free checked bag with Basic Economy or the lowest fare?

No — the lowest fare tiers on Wizz Air generally don't include a free checked bag; it's a separate add-on regardless of fare class.
