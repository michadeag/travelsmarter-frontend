Frequently asked questions

How much does travel insurance typically cost?

Travel insurance typically costs 4% to 12% of your total prepaid, non-refundable trip cost, depending mainly on your age, trip length, and how comprehensive the coverage is.

Is comprehensive coverage worth the extra cost?

For expensive trips, older travelers, or destinations with adventure activities, comprehensive coverage — especially strong emergency medical evacuation limits — is usually worth the roughly 30% premium over a basic policy.

What is cancel-for-any-reason (CFAR) coverage?

CFAR lets you cancel for reasons a standard policy wouldn't cover, but it typically costs 40-60% more than a standard plan, must be purchased within 14-21 days of your first trip deposit, and only reimburses a portion (often 50-75%) of your trip cost.

Do I need travel insurance for a domestic trip?

Often less critical than for international travel, since your existing health insurance usually covers emergency care — but trip cancellation coverage can still be worth it for an expensive, non-refundable domestic trip.
