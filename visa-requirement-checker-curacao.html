Frequently asked questions

Do I need a visa to visit Curaçao as a US citizen?

No traditional visa is required. US citizens can visit Curaçao visa-free for short tourist stays, but must complete an online Digital Immigration Card (DI Card) before arrival.

How long can US citizens stay in Curaçao without a visa?

US citizens can generally stay up to 90 days per visit under the standard tourist entry terms.

Do I need to apply for anything before I fly to Curaçao?

Yes — Digital Immigration Card (DI Card) is required, and should be arranged before you travel, ideally as soon as you have your itinerary.

How long must my passport be valid to visit Curaçao?

A commonly-applied rule is that your passport should remain valid at least 6 months beyond your trip. Always confirm the exact requirement close to your travel date.
