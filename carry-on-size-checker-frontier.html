Frequently asked questions

What is the carry-on size limit for Frontier?

Frontier's carry-on limit is 24" x 16" x 10", with a 35lb weight limit.

Does Frontier charge for carry-on bags?

Yes — only the personal item is free on every Frontier fare. A full-size carry-on is a paid add-on, and it's cheaper to add it during booking than at the gate.

What is Frontier's personal item size limit?

Frontier's personal item limit is 18" x 14" x 8" — it must fit under the seat in front of you, and it's always free.

Does Frontier weigh carry-on bags?

Yes — Frontier enforces a 35lb weight limit on the paid carry-on bag, in addition to the size limit.
