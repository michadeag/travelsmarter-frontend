Frequently asked questions

What is the best month to visit Montreal?

June–September is the best window to visit Montreal. Warm summer weather and a packed festival calendar.

When should I avoid visiting Montreal?

December–February is the stretch to avoid if possible. Very cold, with heavy snow — though appealing if winter sports are the goal.

Is the best time to visit Montreal the same as the cheapest time to fly there?

Not necessarily — weather and crowd timing don't always match the cheapest airfare windows. Check our Best Time to Book Flights tool separately for pricing.

Is shoulder season worth it for Montreal?

Usually yes — the weeks just before or after Montreal's best window often have nearly as good weather with noticeably fewer crowds and lower prices.
