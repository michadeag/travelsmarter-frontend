Why a generic packing list doesn't work

Most packing list templates ignore climate entirely — the same list for a beach trip to Bali and a winter trip to Reykjavik doesn't make sense. This generator adjusts clothing and essentials based on your destination's typical climate (tropical, desert, Mediterranean, temperate, or cold), and scales quantities like underwear and socks to your actual trip length instead of a generic "pack for a week" default.
