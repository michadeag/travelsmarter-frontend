Frequently asked questions

Does Atlanta (ATL) have free WiFi?

Yes. Free WiFi is unlimited and reliable — the airport's biggest challenge is sheer size, not connectivity.

How good are the lounges at Atlanta (ATL)?

Lounge quality here is solid. A day pass, often purchasable without status or a premium ticket, can be well worth it for a longer layover.

What's the one amenity worth seeking out at Atlanta (ATL)?

The Delta Sky Club network here is one of the most extensive in the US.

Is Atlanta (ATL) a good airport for a long layover?

It depends on how long you have — check the amenity above, and otherwise treat it as a functional connection rather than a destination in itself.
