What is the minimum connection time at Las Vegas (LAS)?

How much extra time should I add at Las Vegas (LAS) for a comfortable connection?

Add about 30 minutes on top of the minimum connection time for a comfortable buffer — so roughly 75 minutes for domestic-to-domestic, 120 minutes for a mixed connection, or 105 minutes for international-to-international.

What should I do if my connection at Las Vegas (LAS) is too tight?

Check in online and get your boarding pass before landing if possible, go straight to your gate if you only have carry-on luggage, and if you do miss the connection, head to your airline's transfer desk or app rather than a general check-in line for faster rebooking.
