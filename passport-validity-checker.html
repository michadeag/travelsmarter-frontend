The rule most travelers only learn the hard way

Most travelers check their passport's expiry date against their return date — but that's the wrong comparison. Many countries require your passport to stay valid for 6 months beyond your entry date, or 3 months beyond your departure for Schengen countries, and airlines enforce this at check-in, before you ever reach immigration.
