Flying to Zurich from the US

Zurich Airport (ZRH) is one of the better-connected Swiss gateways from the US, with nonstop flights from JFK, EWR, ORD, SFO, LAX, and BOS on Swiss International Air Lines, United, Delta, and American. Flight times run roughly 8 to 8.5 hours eastbound. There's no dedicated low-cost transatlantic carrier flying this route nonstop — "budget" fares here mostly mean the lowest economy class on a full-service airline, not a separate no-frills operator.

Summer (June–August) is peak season, driven by both Alpine tourism and Zurich's role as a business and finance hub. Ski season (December–March) brings a second demand bump, especially around the holidays and February school breaks, as travelers connect through Zurich to Swiss resorts. Shoulder months — April–May and September–October — offer the best mix of good weather and lower fares.

Budget vs. premium carriers on this route

Because there's no true budget carrier on the nonstop Zurich route, "budget" fares are really discounted economy seats released by Swiss, United, Delta, or American — and these sell out early. Waiting rarely helps for the lowest-priced seats; booking 3–5 weeks out tends to catch what's left of the cheap inventory, while 4–6 weeks out is the sweet spot for premium economy and business-class fares before airlines raise prices closer to departure.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to Zurich for the cheapest fare?

4–6 weeks out on full-service carriers, or 3–5 weeks out for the lowest discounted economy fares. Zurich has no dedicated budget transatlantic carrier, so the cheapest seats on Swiss, United, Delta, or American sell early.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to Zurich?

November and January–March (outside the February ski break) are typically cheapest. Summer and ski season both push fares up, so April–May and September–October give you the best balance of value and weather.

Is it cheaper to fly to Zurich on a budget or full-service airline?

There's no dedicated low-cost carrier on this route — it comes down to which fare class Swiss, United, Delta, or American has released. Booking 4–6 weeks out generally beats waiting until the last minute.

Do I need a visa to visit Zurich from the US?

US citizens can currently visit visa-free for up to 90 days in the Schengen area. The EU's ETIAS travel authorization has been rolling out — check the current requirement close to your travel date.
