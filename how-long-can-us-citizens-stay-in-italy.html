How Long Can US Citizens Stay in Italy?

US citizens can stay in Italy for up to 90 days without a visa for tourism. This is the shared Schengen limit: 90 days within any 180-day period, counted across all Schengen countries combined — not per country. Time spent elsewhere in Schengen reduces your Italy allowance.

Staying longer than 90 days

The 90/180 rule is rolling, not calendar-based: every day you are in the Schengen area during the previous 180 days counts against your limit. For a stay in Italy beyond 90 days you would need a national long-stay visa (work, study, family or — in several EU countries — a digital nomad visa), applied for at a consulate before travel. Border-hopping to a neighbouring Schengen country does not reset the clock.

One detail that catches travelers out: Italy expects your passport to remain valid for 3 months beyond your planned stay. Airlines enforce this at check-in, so a passport expiring soon can end a trip before it starts — check yours before booking.

What happens if you overstay?

Overstaying in Italy — even by a few days — can mean fines, being flagged in the immigration system, and trouble on future entries. Some countries also bar re-entry for a period proportional to the overstay. The safe play is simple: count your days from the entry stamp (not your booking dates), set a reminder a week before your limit, and if plans change, deal with immigration before the deadline rather than after.

Frequently asked questions

Does the 90-day limit reset if I leave and come back?

No — for Schengen countries the 90/180 rule keeps counting across borders, so a quick exit does not reset anything.

Do I need anything before I fly to Italy?

Yes — ETIAS (rolling out). Arrange it before departure; airlines check at the gate.

Can I work remotely from Italy as a tourist?

Working for a foreign employer while visiting is a legal grey zone in most countries. For anything beyond answering emails on vacation, check the digital nomad visa options for Italy — many destinations now have a dedicated permit for exactly this.
