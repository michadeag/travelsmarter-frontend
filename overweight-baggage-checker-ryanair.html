Frequently asked questions

What's the overweight baggage fee on Ryanair?

Over the 20kg (44lb) allowance: charged per kilo, often €10-12/kg if paid at the gate — one of the steepest overage rates of any major carrier.

What happens if my bag is way over the limit on Ryanair?

Over 32kg (70lb): not accepted at all by most ground handlers, for baggage-handling safety reasons.

What's the oversized baggage fee on Ryanair?

Oversized or odd-shaped items (sports equipment, musical instruments) need a separate special-item booking, not just an oversize fee.

How do I avoid this fee on Ryanair?

Pre-booking extra weight online is dramatically cheaper than paying the per-kilo gate rate — do it before you travel, not at check-in.
