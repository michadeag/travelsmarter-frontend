Frequently asked questions

How many time zones is Siem Reap from the US?

It depends on where you're departing from: anywhere from 7 hours (from US Hawaii) up to 12 hours (from US Eastern), since Siem Reap is a fixed offset from UTC and the gap depends on your home time zone.

Is jet lag worse flying to Siem Reap or coming home?

Generally the direction with more hours crossed eastward is worse, since your body adjusts to losing time (advancing the clock) more slowly than gaining it (delaying the clock). Check the table above for your specific route's direction.

How long does jet lag last when flying to Siem Reap?

As a rough rule of thumb, plan on about 5 to 12 days to fully adjust, depending on your departure time zone and how much daylight exposure you get on arrival.

What's the best way to reduce jet lag flying to Siem Reap?

Start shifting your sleep schedule a day or two before departure, stay hydrated and limit alcohol on the flight, set your watch to Siem Reap time as soon as you board, and get outside into daylight as soon as you land to help reset your body clock faster.
