    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need vaccines to visit Spain?

    Commonly recommended items for Spain include: routine vaccines up to date. This is general orientation, not medical advice — confirm with a travel clinic 4-6 weeks before departure.

    Is yellow fever vaccination required for Spain?

    Spain does not commonly require yellow fever vaccination for entry from the US, though it may still be recommended for certain regions within the country — check the specific area on your itinerary.

    What is the malaria risk in Spain?

    No malaria risk.

    When should I get travel vaccines before visiting Spain?

    Visit a travel medicine clinic or your doctor 4-6 weeks before departure — some vaccines need time to become effective or require multiple doses spaced weeks apart.
