Frequently asked questions

Does United Airlines charge extra for skis or a snowboard?

Counts as one checked bag, no extra fee. Ski and snowboard equipment is treated as a standard checked bag if within the normal 50 lb weight limit.

Does United Airlines charge extra for golf clubs?

Counts as one checked bag, no extra fee. Golf clubs get the same treatment as ski equipment — a standard checked bag if within the normal 50 lb weight limit.

Does United Airlines charge extra for a surfboard?

~$200 special item fee. Applies to boards over standard checked-bag dimensions — check current size cutoffs, as they're periodically adjusted.

Does United Airlines charge extra for a bike?

~$200 special item fee. The bike must be boxed or in a proper bike bag — loose bikes are not accepted.
