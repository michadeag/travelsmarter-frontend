Frequently asked questions

What am I owed if Ryanair loses my bag?

You may be owed up to about 1,288 sdr (roughly $1,700-$1,800, fluctuating with exchange rates). This is the Montreal Convention liability limit for international carriage, which applies to every carrier here. It's a cap, not a guarantee — the airline can pay less based on your bag and contents' documented value.

What if my bag arrives damaged on Ryanair?

You may be owed up to about 1,288 sdr (roughly $1,700-$1,800, fluctuating with exchange rates). Same Montreal Convention cap applies to damage as to loss. You must report damage within 7 days of receiving the bag to preserve your claim — this deadline is much stricter than for a delayed or lost bag.

How does Ryanair handle baggage claims?

Ryanair routes all baggage claims through its online claims portal. The Montreal Convention sets tight deadlines — 7 days to report damage, 21 days for a delayed bag — so file promptly to keep your claim valid.

Which rules apply to Ryanair?

Ryanair's international flights are covered by the Montreal Convention, the treaty that sets baggage liability limits worldwide.
