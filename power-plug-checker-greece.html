Frequently asked questions

What plug type does Greece use?

Greece uses Type C (two round pins, ungrounded ("Europlug")), Type F (two round pins plus two ground clips ("Schuko", German style)).

Do I need a plug adapter for Greece from the US?

Yes — Greece's outlets use a different shape than US plugs, so you'll need a plug adapter.

What voltage does Greece run on?

Greece runs on 230V at 50Hz, notably different from the US standard of 120V — check that your device is rated for this voltage before plugging in directly.

Do I need a voltage converter for Greece?

Most modern electronics (phone and laptop chargers) are dual-voltage (100-240V) and only need a plug adapter, not a converter. Single-voltage, high-wattage devices like hair dryers or straighteners may need an actual voltage converter — check the label near the plug prongs for the input voltage range.
