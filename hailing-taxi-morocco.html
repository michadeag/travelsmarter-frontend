How Do You Hail a Taxi in Morocco?

In Morocco, hailing a taxi by raising your hand on the street is the standard method in cities, and it works reliably. Designated taxi stands exist near major landmarks and transport hubs but are not required — street hailing is completely normal.

Morocco has two distinct taxi types: petit taxis (small, colored by city, for trips within city limits) and grand taxis (larger, for intercity routes or group hire, usually found at fixed stands). Petit taxis are the ones you'll typically hail on the street day-to-day. Ride-hailing apps like Careem and inDrive are available in larger cities such as Casablanca and Marrakech and are increasingly popular, especially among younger locals and tourists who want upfront pricing. Phoning ahead is uncommon for casual trips but some hotels can arrange taxis on request.

💡 Agree on the fare or insist the driver use the meter (compteur) before you get in — meters are legally required for petit taxis but drivers sometimes try to negotiate a flat rate instead, which is often higher.

Frequently asked questions

Do Moroccan taxi drivers speak English?

In major tourist cities like Marrakech, Fes, and Casablanca, many petit taxi drivers have basic English or French. French is far more widely spoken than English, so knowing a few French phrases or having your destination written down is genuinely helpful in smaller cities or with older drivers.

Is Uber available in Morocco?

Uber does not operate in Morocco. Careem (owned by Uber) and inDrive are the main ride-hailing alternatives and work in cities like Casablanca, Rabat, and Marrakech. These apps are useful because fares are agreed upfront, removing any haggling.

What is a grand taxi and when would I use one?

Grand taxis are typically older Mercedes sedans that operate fixed intercity or longer routes, departing from specific stands when they have a full load of passengers — usually six. They are cheap but you share the ride with strangers and wait until the car fills up. For private intercity hire you can negotiate to pay for all seats yourself.
