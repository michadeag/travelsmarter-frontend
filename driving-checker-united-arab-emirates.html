Frequently asked questions

Which side of the road does United Arab Emirates drive on?

United Arab Emirates drives on the right-hand side of the road.

Do I need an International Driving Permit for United Arab Emirates?

Not strictly, but it's recommended. The UAE generally accepts US licenses for visitors and rental cars, though carrying an IDP is recommended as backup ID.

Where do I get an International Driving Permit before I travel?

In the US, an IDP is issued by AAA or the National Auto Club — it costs around $20, requires a passport photo, and can't be obtained after you've already left the country.

Is an IDP a license by itself?

No — an IDP is a translation document, not a standalone license. You must carry your regular US driver's license alongside it at all times.
