Frequently asked questions

What is the carry-on size limit for Ryanair?

Ryanair's priority cabin bag limit is 21.5" x 15.5" x 7.75" (55 x 40 x 20cm), with a 10kg (22lb) weight limit, and requires purchasing Priority boarding in advance.

Does Ryanair charge for carry-on bags?

Yes — only a small underseat bag is free on every fare. The larger cabin bag requires paid Priority boarding, and gate fees for non-compliant bags are significantly higher than booking ahead.

What is Ryanair's personal item (small bag) size limit?

Ryanair's free small bag limit is 15.75" x 7.75" x 9.75" (40 x 20 x 25cm) — it must fit under the seat, and there's no weight limit on this smaller free bag.

Does Ryanair weigh carry-on bags?

Yes — the larger priority cabin bag has a strictly enforced 10kg (22lb) weight limit, checked at the gate alongside size sizers.
