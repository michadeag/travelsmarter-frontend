Flying to Prague from the US

Prague (PRG) currently has no nonstop passenger service from the US — every itinerary connects through a European hub, most commonly Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Paris, or London, on Lufthansa, KLM, Air France, British Airways, or their US partners American, Delta, and United. Total travel time typically runs 11 to 14 hours including the connection, depending on layover length.

December brings a well-known fare and hotel-price spike around Prague's Christmas markets, one of the most popular in Europe, alongside cold weather and short daylight hours. Peak season (June–August) sees the highest fares outside the holidays and the biggest crowds around the Charles Bridge and Old Town. Shoulder season (April–May and September–October) offers the best balance of mild weather, thinner crowds, and lower fares.

Budget vs. premium carriers on this route

Because Prague has no nonstop US service and no dedicated low-cost transatlantic operator, full-service carriers and their connecting itineraries set the price. Booking about a month out, and comparing a few different hub connections, generally beats both booking too early and waiting until the last minute.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to Prague for the cheapest fare?

4–6 weeks out on full-service carriers, or 3–5 weeks out if your connection has flexibility. With no nonstop option, comparing total itinerary price and layover matters as much as raw timing.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to Prague?

January–March and November are cheapest, outside a December spike for the Christmas markets. April–May and September–October balance good weather with lower fares than summer.

Is it cheaper to fly to Prague on a budget or full-service airline?

There's no dedicated budget carrier and no nonstop US service — you'll connect via Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Paris, or London on a full-service carrier.

Do I need a visa to visit Prague from the US?

US citizens can visit the Czech Republic visa-free for up to 90 days in the Schengen area. Check the current ETIAS requirement before booking.
