Flying to Dublin from the US

Dublin (DUB) is one of the best-connected Irish gateways in Europe for US travelers, with nonstop flights from JFK, EWR, ORD, BOS, ATL, and SFO, plus additional seasonal routes from other hubs, on Aer Lingus, Delta, United, American, and JetBlue. Eastbound flight times run about 6.5 to 7.5 hours depending on departure city, among the shortest transatlantic crossings to continental Europe.

Peak season (June–August) brings the highest fares and the busiest immigration queues at Dublin Airport, especially since Dublin operates US preclearance, which adds time on the outbound leg. Shoulder season — April–May and September–October — offers a good balance of mild weather and lower fares, while winter (November–March, outside the holidays) is the cheapest stretch overall, if grey and rainy.

Budget vs. premium carriers on this route

Unlike some European routes, Dublin doesn't have a dedicated ultra-low-cost transatlantic operator — Aer Lingus, Delta, United, American, and JetBlue all compete directly on price. Fares tend to firm up in the final 2–3 weeks before departure, so booking a month or so out usually beats both booking too early and waiting until the last minute.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to Dublin for the cheapest fare?

4–6 weeks out on full-service carriers, or 3–5 weeks out for the leanest fares. Dublin fares are competitive but firm up quickly inside 3 weeks of departure.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to Dublin?

January–March and November are cheapest. April–May and September–October balance good weather with lower fares than the June–August peak.

Is it cheaper to fly to Dublin on a budget or full-service airline?

There's no dedicated ultra-low-cost carrier on this route — Aer Lingus, Delta, United, American, and JetBlue all compete on price, so timing matters more than airline choice.

Do I need a visa to visit Dublin from the US?

No. Ireland isn't in the Schengen area — US citizens can visit visa-free for up to 90 days, and no advance authorization is currently required.
