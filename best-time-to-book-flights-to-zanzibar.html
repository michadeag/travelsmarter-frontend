Flying to Zanzibar from the US

There's no nonstop service to Zanzibar from the US — travelers connect through Europe or the Middle East (Doha, Amsterdam, Istanbul), with total journey time of 20+ hours.

Dry seasons (June–October and December–February) have the best beach weather and are the priciest. The long rains (March–May) are the cheapest but wettest period.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book flights to Zanzibar for the cheapest fare?

6 to 8 weeks out on full-service carriers, or 5 to 7 weeks out on lower fare classes.

What is the cheapest time of year to fly to Zanzibar?

The long rains (March–May) are the cheapest but wettest period. The dry seasons (June–October, December–February) have the best weather and are the priciest.

Is it cheaper to fly to Zanzibar on a budget or full-service airline?

Every itinerary connects since there's no nonstop option — the routing and partner airline matter more than a simple budget-vs-premium choice.

Do I need a visa to visit Zanzibar from the US?

US citizens need a visa for Tanzania, available as an e-Visa online in advance or a visa on arrival for a fee — it is not visa-free.
