Frequently asked questions

Is Turkey wheelchair accessible?

Turkey: Moderate Accessibility. Modern hotels and Istanbul's newer transit lines are generally accessible, alongside real gaps in older infrastructure and historic sites.

What does "Moderate Accessibility" mean?

Accessible pockets exist (resorts, newer developments, major hotels) alongside significant general infrastructure gaps — research your specific itinerary carefully.

Is this a guarantee for a specific hotel or attraction?

This is general, national-level orientation based on infrastructure investment patterns — not a guarantee for any specific hotel, venue, or transit route, and accessibility needs vary a lot by disability type. Always verify accessibility directly with specific hotels, attractions, and transit providers for your exact itinerary before you book.

What should I do before booking?

Call or email specific hotels and attractions directly to confirm accessibility for your exact needs, book airline/airport wheelchair assistance in advance rather than at the airport, and consider an accessible-travel specialist tour operator for your destination.
