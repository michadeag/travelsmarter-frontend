Frequently asked questions

What public holidays does Nigeria observe?

Nigeria's public holidays include New Year's Day, Independence Day, Christmas Day, plus movable dates like Eid al-Fitr.

Will banks and shops be closed in Nigeria during a public holiday?

Banks, government offices, and many local shops commonly close on public holidays in Nigeria — worth planning cash withdrawals and paperwork around them.

Do public holiday dates in Nigeria change every year?

Some do — Eid al-Fitr shift every year based on the lunar or Easter-based calendar, so always confirm the exact date for your travel year.

Should I avoid traveling to Nigeria during a public holiday?

Not necessarily — some travelers enjoy the festive atmosphere, but expect closures at some businesses and potentially packed transport or fully booked restaurants as locals travel too.
