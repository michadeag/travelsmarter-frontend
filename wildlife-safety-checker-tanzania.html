Frequently asked questions

What are the wildlife safety risks in Tanzania?

Tanzania's wildlife hazard level is high — notable venomous or dangerous wildlife is a real consideration for outdoor activities. Safari and Kilimanjaro-adjacent areas have dangerous large mammals — always follow guide instructions on safari.

What should I do if bitten or stung in Tanzania?

Stay calm, keep the affected limb still and below heart level if possible, and get to medical care immediately — never attempt to suck out venom or apply a tourniquet, both are outdated and can cause more harm.

Is it safe to hike or camp in Tanzania?

Take extra precautions — wear closed-toe shoes on trails, check bedding and shoes in rural accommodation, and book guided tours for wildlife-viewing activities.

Should I book a guided tour for wildlife areas in Tanzania?

Yes, especially for safari, jungle, or rainforest activities — guides know current local hazards and how to respond to them, and significantly reduce risk compared to exploring independently.
