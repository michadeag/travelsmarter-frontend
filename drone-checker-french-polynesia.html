Frequently asked questions

Can I fly my drone in French Polynesia?

Drones in French Polynesia are restricted — permits required for most areas. Drone flying requires registration, and many resort lagoons restrict drones — check with your resort or the relevant authority before flying.

Do I need to register my drone before visiting French Polynesia?

Yes — register before you fly, not after you land. Few authorities offer same-day approval, so this needs to happen before your trip.

Will my drone be confiscated at customs in French Polynesia?

Unlikely if you've completed the required registration, but keep your registration certificate or permit accessible in case customs asks.

Are there photography restrictions in French Polynesia even without a drone?

Yes — photographing military sites, government buildings, and some infrastructure can be restricted almost anywhere in the world, drone or no drone. When in doubt, don't photograph it.
